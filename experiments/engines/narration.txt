Just remembering a big win can raise goosebumps all over your body. Cold can do it, and so can fear, music, or a horror film. Tiny muscles at the base of your hairs contract and pull them straight up, a reflex started by your fight-or-flight nerves. Here's the twist: feeling chills and getting bumps are not the same thing. In research, the bumps people reported didn't always match the bumps measured. In furry animals, raised hair traps warm air. Frightened cats and chimpanzees bristle to look bigger. Porcupines raise their quills when threatened. And some people can raise goosebumps on purpose, no trigger needed. Many don't realize not everyone can. Some call it a leftover reflex, but it may be your body's sign that you were moved.
