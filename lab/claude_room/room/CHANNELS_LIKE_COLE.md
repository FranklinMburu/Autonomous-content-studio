## What this says
1. **Channel size doesn't matter.** Channels with 552 to 8K subscribers get 0.3–5M. The format and the topic carry it.
2. **Since launch (19 Nov), useful content wins:** money methods, secret locations, "things you didn't know", "do this first". These get the highest like rates (3–7%) and the fastest growth, about 1M+ in 24–48 h. People now own the game and search for help.
3. **Very short comparisons (6–21 s) get the biggest raw numbers** (9.3M, 5.2M, 2.9M, 2.2M), because they loop. Their like rates are low (~1%).
4. **Explainers ("How X works in GTA 6") get 1.6–1.9M** at 37–64 s.
5. **The titles are plain and literal,** not clever: "5 Things I Didn't Know About GTA 6", "How The Wanted System Works".
