## What it means
1. Three Shorts made 96% of views. Ideas that work: irony (13 years → settings menu), a list of discoveries, a money tip. Single streamer reactions die in the test pool (under 50 impressions, 1–12 views): stop making them unless one carries a strong idea.
2. The first second is the bottleneck: 41–57% stay. The parrot is watched to 77% once people stay, but 59% swipe at once, so the content is good and frame 0 plus the opening words lose them. Fix: open on the payoff (money counter / reveal) with the value said in the first 0.5 s.
3. No comments, almost no shares or subs: no question at the end, no pinned comment. Add a natural question ending and a pinned comment on every Short.
4. Endings don't loop (no bump at the end of the curve). Make the last frame flow into the first.
5. The audience is largely Portuguese-speaking (Brazil plus Portugal outweigh the US 9:1). Test Portuguese on-screen text/titles on one money tip, and Portuguese subtitles. Campaigns that need 50% US/UK/CA/AU/NZ viewers (Reach's Lacy and Clix) are out of reach; the Kai pool has no country rule.
6. Search is small but real ("gta 6 kaicenat"): put the exact names people search ("GTA 6", the streamer) at the start of titles.
