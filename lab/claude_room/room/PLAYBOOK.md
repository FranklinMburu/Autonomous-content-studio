## 2. Title formulas
**Same moment, different title:**
- Basketball: "Kai Cenat **Shoots a Basketball for the FIRST Time** in GTA 6! 😮‍💨🏀" **274K** vs "Kai Cenat tries basketball in GTA 6 🏀" **14K**. The winner has a concrete action verb, "FIRST Time" in caps, an exclamation mark and two emoji. "Tries" is weak.
- Cops: "Cop Chase Goes WRONG 😭" 14K vs "Did Kai Cenat Really Just Lose The Cops?" 4.7K. **Question titles lose.**

**Formulas that win:**
- `[Streamer] [concrete verb] [ordinary object/action] for the FIRST Time in GTA 6! [2 emoji]`
- `[Streamer] Was [emotion] He Couldn't [expected thing] In GTA 6!`
- `[Streamer] Spent [NUMBER] MINUTES [mundane task] in GTA 6 😭🙏`

Rules:
- Put the "wait what?" in the **object**, not the adjective.
- Use one to three words in CAPS at most.
- Use two emoji at the end (😭 💀 😳 🏀).
- Put the streamer's name first.
- Do not phrase it as a question.
- Do not use lowercase all the way through.

**Losers:** vague ("Found GTA 6's Real Upgrade" 15K), abstract ("IT'S NOT BROKEN CODE" 19K), moralizing (vape PSA 2.4K), generic verbs (drives, gym: under 1.5K).

## 5. Critic checklist (score 0-2 each, ship at 14 or above out of 20)
1. The angle is one of #2-#9, not "first time playing" or generic shock.
2. No more than two other Shorts of this exact moment exist.
3. The title names a concrete object, action or number, not just an emotion.
4. Streamer name first, "GTA 6" included, not a question.
5. One to three CAPS words and one or two emoji at the end.
6. The "wait what?" lands in the first 1.5s: the game event is visible and the streamer's face is on screen.
7. Under 40s, ending on the payoff line or reaction (loopable).
8. A stranger with no context understands it.
9. Captions are burned in, the key quote included.
10. The clip is cut and transformed (zoom, captions, face cam), not a raw VOD re-upload (DMCA risk).
