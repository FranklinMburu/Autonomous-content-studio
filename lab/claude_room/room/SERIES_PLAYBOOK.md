# GTA 6 Shorts SERIES PLAYBOOK (20 Nov 2026)

## 1. Evidence
- **Each part must pay off on its own.** Paddy Galloway says Shorts are like intros, but "they need to have a conclusion" ([EssentiallySports](https://www.essentiallysports.com/esports-news-shortz-are-like-intros-that-need-to-have-secret-content-consultant-of-mrbeast-shares-the-formula-of-making-perfect-youtube-shortz)). The feed doesn't serve parts in order.
- **Themed series work when every episode stands alone.** When one episode takes off it "naturally encourages viewers to check out the other related videos" ([YouTube blog](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/cracking-the-code-to-youtube-shorts-part-2/)).
- **YouTube is pushing episodic Shorts.** "Shorts series" (seasons, episodes, sequential playback) launched on 23 Sep 2026, but **only for YPP channels** ([YouTube blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/made-on-youtube-creators-shorts-series-tv-features/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/23/youtubes-new-short-series-feature-brings-episodic-viewing-to-shorts/)). At 300 subs, we can't use it yet.
- **The linking tool we can use** is the **Related video** link. It can point to another Short and shows as a clickable link under the channel handle. It needs advanced features ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157)). Links in Shorts comments and descriptions are not clickable ([SEJ](https://searchenginejournal.com/youtube-adds-shorts-links-limits-links-elsewhere/495463)).
- **Parts lose views.** Kai's GTA 6 stream got 11.75M for Part 1 and 6.05M for Part 2, about 51% (TREND_DATA_19NOV, long-form). No public Shorts Part 1 vs 2 data found; Ten'O'Clock's Part 2 couldn't be fetched (YouTube blocked here). Check it by hand.
- **What wins this week (CHANNELS_LIKE_COLE):** useful, plain titles ("5 Things You Didn't Know About GTA 6", 1M+ in 24 h).
- **Freshness matters.** Shorts older than about 30 days reportedly lose impressions. This is unconfirmed ([SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-shorts-algorithm-may-now-favor-fresh-over-evergreen/562661/)).
- **Cliffhanger craft:** give a payoff first, then leave ONE open question in the last 3 to 6 s. Bait-and-switch and padding destroy trust ([CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/create/cliffhanger-endings-short-form-video-series)). Length: 13 s or 60 s tend to perform best ([vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)).

## 2. Series template
Use **"GTA 6 Secrets Nobody Told You #N"** for a run of standalone tips, and **"[Streamer]'s Worst Day in GTA 6 (Part N)"** for a short story arc.
- 0-1 s: payoff visual plus "#N" tag. Then one complete secret or moment with the reaction.
- Last 2-3 s: a hard cut to a 1 s **flash of the next part's best frame** plus text: "#N+1: a DOG drives you home".
- Length: 15-35 s for each clip. Use about 60 s only for a "3 secrets" compilation.

## 3. Cliffhanger and ending mechanics
- **Do:** finish the reveal, THEN tease the next one. Tease text must read with sound off.
- **Do:** after posting Part N+1, edit Part N's Related video to point to it. Point Part N+1 back to Part 1. The pinned comment should say "Part 2 is the link under my name", with no URL.
- **Don't:** cut off mid-reveal. Viewers feel cheated, and misleading content is banned ([Spam policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973)).
- **Don't:** post "Part 2 at 1K likes". At our size it won't hit. Ask for a follow instead.

## 4. Cadence
- Post Part 2 **2-24 h after Part 1**, sooner if Part 1 is climbing.
- Max one series Short per three uploads.
- Cap a series at 3-5 parts. Expect each part to get about half the views of the one before. If Part 2 gets under 25% of Part 1, end the series and go back to standalone Shorts.

## 5. Risks
- Most viewers see one part; a part needing context dies.
- A streamer-clip series compounds DMCA exposure. Transform every clip.
- "Secrets" must be true: verify each mechanic in the clip.
- Over-teasing reads as padding (CapCut).
