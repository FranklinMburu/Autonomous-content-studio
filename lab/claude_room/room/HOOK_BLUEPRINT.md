# HOOK BLUEPRINT (20 Nov)

**Evidence.** Galloway (3.3B Shorts views): best Shorts are 70-90% viewed-vs-swiped, so "treat your intro like a thumbnail." MrBeast: the opening must meet the packaging's promise.

## Rules (this week's proof)
1. **The noun is the hook.** Alligator 233K, Bigfoot 152K, autosave 175K. "Jynxzi LOSES IT" got 3 views, from the same streamer.
2. **Action verb, not a mood.** Barking and found win. "Crashed out" was Kai's low (46K).
3. **Give it a turn.** "Instantly Regrets", "...Then Said Sorry", "...Then Refused To Restart".
4. **Write it like a text.** "Kai Cenat Finds Out You Can SHOWER in GTA 6" sounds like a friend. "NPCs ARE TOO REAL" sounds like an ad.
5. **Keep it true.** Every word should be visible or audible in the clip.
6. **Name first, then GTA 6, then 1-2 emoji, no question.** Emoji don't rescue "LOSES IT 😡".
7. **Nothing passive or abstract.** "Watches Lucia crash" 29K, "Only 20% Into" 9.4K.

## Patterns
- `[Streamer] [verb] [specific thing] In GTA 6… Then [consequence] 😭[+1]`
- `[Streamer] Found Out You Can [mundane act] In GTA 6 [emoji]`

## Red flags
TOO REAL, INSANE, goes crazy, LOSES IT, COOKED, "Streamers React", "Rockstar went too far", unfillable lists ("5 RULES"), abstract nouns (realism, hygiene, details), all-caps lines, questions, 3+ emoji, narrator voice.

## On-screen text vs title
- **On-screen:** for viewers already watching, sound off. 7 words or fewer, lowercase speech, one caps word at most. The tension shown in frame 0, never the payoff.
- **Title:** for search and the feed. The name, GTA 6 and the full story with its "then...", without repeating the on-screen text.

## 30-second rubric (1 point each, ship at 8+)
1. Would a friend text it?
2. Is every claim in the footage?
3. Is there a concrete noun?
4. Is there a concrete verb?
5. Is there a gap or turn?
6. Does a stranger get it?
7. Is the on-screen text 7 words or fewer, with no payoff?
8. Does the title go name first, GTA 6, no question?
9. Are there zero red flags?
10. Does frame 0 show what the text names?
