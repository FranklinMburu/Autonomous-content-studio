  - oakgen.ai read the CR pages on 19 Nov.
  - clippingalpha.com updated its data 19 Nov, 10:18 UTC.
- **Reported (17-18 Nov)** = taken from Content Rewards' own articles (`/articles/gta-6-clipping`, `/articles/get-paid-clip-streamers`).
- **Reported (older)** = taken from clyper.io (checked 23 Oct to 16 Nov) or another secondhand source.
- **Before posting, open each link and read the brief.** Budgets drain fast and rules sit behind "Join".

Standard Content Rewards terms apply unless a row says otherwise:
- 10% fee is taken from every per-view payout.
- Ad disclosure goes at the start of each clip.
- Submit the clip within 30 minutes of posting.
- No bought or botted views (permanent ban).
- Do not submit the same cut to two campaigns.
- Do not use IG Collab posts unless the brief allows it.
- Payouts come in about 10-day rounds (7 days earning + 3-day hold), paid to your CR balance, then your Whop balance.

Abbreviations: YT = YouTube Shorts, TT = TikTok, IG = Instagram Reels, X = X/Twitter. "Tier-1 50%" = at least 50% of your audience in the US, CA, UK, AU or NZ.

## Account requirements (short)

- **Content Rewards / Whop:**
  - Connect the TikTok, IG, YouTube, X or Facebook account you post from. Ownership is verified.
  - No follower minimum on open campaigns. Application campaigns screen your account and may ask for a sample.
  - Reach-run streamer campaigns (Lacy, Clix, Speed) require at least 50% of your audience in the US, CA, UK, AU or NZ. Many brand campaigns need 30-50% US. A Kenya-based account with a mostly African audience can have clips rejected, so focus on pool and campaign rows with no audience rule.
  - Some campaigns need a dedicated, themed account (Lauren Hamden, Dasgasd3m, Brez).
  - No account-age rule was found in any source.
- **Vyro:** Ad disclosure is required. Views count only after about 5K (darebay). Earnings stay pending until the campaign ends and views are checked.

## Getting paid from Kenya (not confirmed; the Whop payout docs could not be read)

- **Content Rewards:** Your balance withdraws to your Whop balance; Whop is the payout processor. Whop's "Payout methods" doc lists the options for each country, but it could not be read today. Open Whop, go to Settings, then Payouts, and see what Kenya gets.
  - ClipToCash and sove7gn guides suggest African clippers usually withdraw by bank/wire, crypto (USDC) or PayPal where offered. "Not available in your region" errors do happen.
  - Before relying on it, make a test withdrawal of about $10.
- **Vyro:** Weekly withdrawals from a $10 minimum, by Stripe or PayPal (clyper.io).
  - From Kenya, PayPal is the practical route: PayPal Kenya can withdraw to M-Pesa.
  - Stripe Connect payouts to Kenyan accounts are generally not supported, so use PayPal.
- **Clipster:** $50 minimum, 5% fee.
