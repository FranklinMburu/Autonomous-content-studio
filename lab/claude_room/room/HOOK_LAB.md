### What's keeping the top 2 below 9.4
**#1:** (a) Frame 0 has to show the crime: the NPC walking past the sink, with a red circle on the untouched sink and Kai's "NO!" audio in the first 0.3 s. (b) Name the act instead of judging it: "HE DIDN'T WASH HIS HANDS", with WASH in red. (c) Narrator: "This NPC skipped the sink... watch what GTA 6 does." (d) Keep WASH HANDS and Focus buffed as the payoff at around 70%.
**#4:** (a) Frame 0 should be the mid-air jump with the fire behind him, not the cockpit. (b) Text: "PLANE'S ON FIRE. JUMP.", with FIRE in orange. (c) Narrator: "GTA 6 lets you bail out of a burning plane..." and then the "double parachute" line is the payoff. (d) Cut every N3on reference out of the text. Even fixed, it tops out around 8, because the plane trend is already crowded.
